# AD Arcade 1.9.0 - M5Burner listings (English only)

Notes for the listings:
- Upload one .bin per listing (merged image, flashed at 0x0 by M5Burner). Files are in the GitHub release v1.9.0 and in `release/`.
- Covers (this folder): 1280x720 and 800x800, one pair per device:
  - M5StickC Plus2: `AD-Arcade-cover-1280x720.png` / `AD-Arcade-cover-800x800.png`
  - M5StickC Plus: `AD-Arcade-StickCPlus-cover-1280x720.png` / `...-800x800.png`
  - M5StickS3: `AD-Arcade-StickS3-cover-1280x720.png` / `...-800x800.png`
  - M5Cardputer: `AD-Arcade-Cardputer-cover-1280x720.png` / `...-800x800.png`
- WARNING: M5Burner rejected the StickC Plus build before ("failed to boot the device"). Only upload a build to M5Burner after someone has tested it on the real device. Until then, point users of the StickC Plus, StickS3 and Cardputer to the GitHub release.

## 1) AD Arcade for M5StickC Plus2 (main device, tested)
Name: AD Arcade   |   Version: 1.9.0   |   File: AD-Arcade-M5StickCPlus2-v1.9.0.bin
Cover: AD-Arcade-cover-1280x720.png

### Firmware description
```
**AD Arcade for M5StickC Plus2**

>  **Official GitHub**; [AD-Arcade](https://github.com/ADdevx/AD-Arcade-M5StickCPlus2)

*A pocket arcade console for your M5StickC Plus2 with a clean pixel-art look. 18 games including a full adventure, a tower defense and a pinball, all in one firmware. Flash it, press a button and start playing.*

**Games**
Legend of Emberwood (adventure), Pocket Siege (tower defense), Ember Pinball, Chrome Dino, Flap Bird, Meteors, Maze, Breakout, Invaders, Slalom, Stack, Racing, Flight, River, Fish, Tetris, Snake and Lunar Lander.

**Features**

Legend of Emberwood: village, forest, crypt and a 3-phase boss, with auto-save
Pocket Siege: 12 levels in 4 worlds, 4 towers with 3 levels, upgrade shop
Ember Pinball: bumpers, targets, multiplier, ball save and nudging
Tilt controls using the built-in motion sensor, with a guided calibration at first boot
Tilt or buttons in Tetris and Snake
Daily Challenge: one game per day, 3 tries, streak and 7-day history
2 Players mode: pass the device around
Bronze, silver and gold medals
Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
Skins with a live preview screen
Stats screen
Background music in every game, which you can turn off
Music player with classic tunes
Screen rotation (normal or flipped)
English and French
Saved high scores
Settings for brightness, volume, controls, sleep time, date and time

**Controls**
A (M5 button) to select, B (side button) for the second action, PWR to pause. Hold A and B together for about 1 second (or hold PWR) to go back to the menu.
```

### Version notes
```
- New game: Ember Pinball (scrolling table, bumpers, targets, multiplier up to x5)
- New game: Pocket Siege, a tower defense with 12 levels, 4 worlds and an upgrade shop
- New game: Legend of Emberwood, a top-down adventure with a 3-phase boss
- New menu icons
- Tilt or buttons in Tetris and Snake, screen rotation setting
- Bug fixes
```

## 4) AD Arcade for M5Cardputer (and Cardputer-ADV)
Name: AD Arcade for M5Cardputer   |   Version: 1.9.0   |   File: AD-Arcade-M5Cardputer-v1.9.0.bin
Cover: AD-Arcade-Cardputer-cover-1280x720.png

### Firmware description
```
**AD Arcade for M5Cardputer**

>  **Official GitHub**; [AD-Arcade](https://github.com/ADdevx/AD-Arcade-M5StickCPlus2)

*A pocket arcade console for your M5Cardputer and Cardputer-ADV with a clean pixel-art look. 18 games including a full adventure, a tower defense and a pinball, all in one firmware. Flash it, press a key and start playing.*

*This build has not been tested on a real Cardputer yet. The screen rotation and Enter key issues reported on the Cardputer-ADV are fixed in this version. Your feedback is welcome!*

**Games**
Legend of Emberwood (adventure), Pocket Siege (tower defense), Ember Pinball, Chrome Dino, Flap Bird, Meteors, Maze, Breakout, Invaders, Slalom, Stack, Racing, Flight, River, Fish, Tetris, Snake and Lunar Lander.

**Features**

Keyboard controls: , / ; . keys replace tilt on models without a motion sensor (the ADV motion sensor is used when present)
Legend of Emberwood, Pocket Siege and Ember Pinball
Tilt or keys in Tetris and Snake
Daily Challenge, 2 Players mode, medals, skins, stats
Background music in every game and a music player
English and French, saved high scores

**Controls**
Enter or Space (or the Go button) = A, Tab or Ctrl = B, Del or ` = PWR (pause). Hold Del for 1.5 seconds (or Enter and Tab together) to go back to the menu.
```

### Version notes
```
- New games: Ember Pinball, Pocket Siege, Legend of Emberwood
- Fixed: screen rotated by 90 degrees and Enter/Space not working (Cardputer-ADV report)
- Ctrl also works as B
- Community testing build
```
