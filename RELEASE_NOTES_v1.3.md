## AD Arcade v1.3

17 games for the M5StickC Plus2, now with music in every game.

**New**
- Tetris, Snake, Lunar Lander
- Reworked Flap Bird
- Redesigned animated menu
- Background chiptune music in every game (can be turned off)
- 25 public domain tunes in the music player
- Tilt controls adapt to flat or upright play
- Version number in Settings

**Install**
Flash with [M5Burner](https://burner.m5stack.com/Q5BB26), or flash `AD-Arcade-M5StickCPlus2-v1.3.bin` at address `0x0`.

Bugs and ideas welcome in the Issues tab.
