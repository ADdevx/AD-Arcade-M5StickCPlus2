## AD Arcade v1.4

Medals, themes and a new look for Flap Bird.

**New**
- Trophies: bronze, silver and gold medals for every game
- Theme setting to change the menu colours
- New original music for all the games
- About page in Settings with a QR code to my profile
- Redrawn Flap Bird character

**Fixed**
- Lunar Lander now restarts with M5 after game over

**Install**
Flash with [M5Burner](https://burner.m5stack.com/Q5BB26), or flash `AD-Arcade-M5StickCPlus2-v1.4.bin` at address `0x0`.

Bugs and ideas welcome in the Issues tab.
